For parents, teachers & leaders

Turn one story into a full conversation.

You do not need a complicated lesson plan. Use the book as the starting point, then give children more than one way to process what they heard.

1

Read it

Start with the story and let the child notice the character's challenge, choices, and change.

2

Talk about it

Ask what felt familiar, what surprised them, and what they think the character learned.

3

Sing it

Use the companion song to repeat the core idea in a different, memorable format.
