TRY THIS AT STORYTIME

One story. Three ways to grow.

01

Spot a little courage

Pause at a tricky moment. Ask, “What could the character try next?” Share a time you tried something new.

02

Draw your gifts

After reading, draw something you enjoy or are learning to do. Take turns naming a gift you see in one another.

03

Make the message sing

Play the companion song. Choose a favorite line, add a movement, and sing it together.
