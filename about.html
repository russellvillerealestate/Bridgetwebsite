About Bridget Smith

Writing with purpose, faith, and imagination.

Bridget Smith has dedicated her work to sharing God's love through children's books that encourage confidence, creativity, character, and learning.

The heart behind the books

Helping children know they are loved and made with purpose.

With more than 30 years of Bible study and a vivid imagination, Bridget combines Christian principles with engaging, educational storytelling. Her books are designed to meet children where they are and give them language for courage, identity, faith, and growth.

The goal is not simply to entertain. It is to create stories children can return to—stories that help them understand something about the world and something important about themselves.

A clear mission

Faith and learning can belong in the same story.

Bridget's books weave together character lessons, Scripture, science, counting, music, and imagination so the message reaches children from more than one direction.
