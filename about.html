About Bridget Smith

Writing with faith, purpose, and imagination.

Bridget Smith's work is built around one mission: create stories that help children understand they are loved, gifted, capable of growth, and made with purpose.

The heart behind the books

Helping children know they are loved and made with purpose.

With more than 30 years of Bible study and a vivid imagination, Bridget combines Christian principles with engaging, educational storytelling. Her books give children language for courage, identity, faith, growth, and curiosity.

The goal is not simply to entertain. It is to create stories children can return to—stories that help them understand something about the world and something important about themselves.

The approach

More than one lesson in every story.

Bridget's books bring together character, Scripture, academics, music, and imagination so children can encounter the message from more than one direction.
