About Bridget Smith

Writing with purpose and passion.

Bridget Smith has dedicated her work to sharing God's love through children's books. Her vivid imagination and years of biblical study shape stories that encourage children to grow in faith, confidence, creativity, and learning.

Bridget's journey

A passion for faith and creativity.

With more than 30 years of Bible study and a passion for storytelling, Bridget combines Christian principles with imaginative, educational children's literature.

Her mission is simple: help children know that they are loved by God, that their lives have purpose, and that learning can be full of wonder.
