Hello, I'm Bridget.

With more than 30 years of Bible study and a vivid imagination, Bridget brings Christian principles into stories filled with curiosity, learning, and hope.

Her books explore the questions children carry: how to recognize their gifts, navigate change, keep trying, and understand that they are loved.

Through stories, songs, and Scripture Flags, she invites families and educators to keep those conversations going.
